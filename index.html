01

Local economy

Supporting Local Business

Repairing the partnership between Town Hall and our business community

Local businesses are the backbone of Wasaga Beach. They create jobs, support families, sponsor community organizations, and help give our town its character.

As Deputy Mayor, Mike will work to:
  • Create regular opportunities to hear directly from business owners about their day-to-day challenges.
  • Identify unnecessary red tape, delays, and duplication in municipal approval processes.
  • Support dedicated economic-development expertise—through staff or a qualified contractor—focused on business retention, attraction, and growth.
  • Develop an ongoing, constructive dialogue between Council, municipal staff, and the local business community.
  • Ensure affected businesses are consulted early, before major decisions are finalized.

When local businesses can succeed, Wasaga Beach gains more jobs, stronger services, a more vibrant downtown, and a healthier local economy.

02

Open government

Council & the Public

Removing barriers between residents and Town Hall

Open dialogue between Council and residents is essential to good local government. No one should feel shut out of the decisions that shape their neighbourhood, municipal services, or taxes.

Residents deserve practical ways to follow Council's work, ask questions, share concerns, and be heard before important decisions are made.

Mike will advocate for:
  • Open access to official Town communication platforms, with any restrictions based only on clear, published, and consistently applied standards—not disagreement with Council.
  • Regular Council meetings beginning at 6:00 p.m. so more working residents and families can attend.
  • A less complicated, more respectful process for residents who want to make a public delegation.
  • A five-minute open-comment period at every regular Council meeting.
  • Earlier notice and plain-language information so residents can provide meaningful input before major decisions are finalized.

More access. More voices. Better decisions.

03

Responsible government

Taxpayer Accountability

Transparency residents can understand and trust

Wasaga Beach taxpayers work hard for their money. They deserve straightforward information before, during, and after major Council decisions—not vague explanations once the decision has already been made.

True transparency means clearly showing what is being proposed, what it will cost, what could go wrong, and whether the promised results are actually being delivered.

Mike will work to ensure every major decision and project clearly explains:
  • Why the decision or investment is being proposed.
  • What outcomes residents should expect and how success will be measured.
  • What financial, operational, and long-term risks are involved.
  • How the project will be funded, its expected full cost, and where taxpayer dollars are going.
  • How work is progressing and whether timelines, costs, or expected outcomes have changed.
  • Who is accountable for delivering the result and reporting back to the public.
  • Transparency = Accountability!

No spin. No surprises. Just honest answers, responsible spending, and real accountability—because your money and your trust matter.
